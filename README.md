The single line to send back to a younger self, if one line were all you got.

> Skip the wedding, buy the motorcycle, and tell your father you love him before a hospital room has to say it for you.

## Why this line

Three beats, one breath: skip the wedding (the safe script), buy the
motorcycle (the life actually wanted), and tell your father you love him
before a hospital room has to say it for you — the turn that stings. It is
specific enough to be one person's older self speaking from a life lived,
warm rather than sentimental, and it lands without explaining itself. The
final clause does the work of the whole line: it turns two pieces of
reckless advice into a deadline that makes them urgent.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib, no dependencies) writes the line to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), with a
self-check gate (1–200 characters, single line, not whitespace-only) and a
byte-exact read-back verification.
